# Immersive Incant room

The normal view is a full-screen engraved frame, parchment, animated wand, and small illustrated entry points for typed spells and desk tools. The center keeps the original sigil and invitation. Portrait and landscape have separately generated frame illustrations so the creatures do not flatten on rotation. A feathered foreground layer lets foliage and creatures overlap the drawing/result visually. It is decorative only: exported sketches and saved generated images contain no frame or UI. The complete original stroke data remains intact beneath the display treatment.

Tap or hold the wand retains the verified voice gesture handling. The wand draws back while listening; finishing/casting drives a brief forward motion, traveling spell and radiating lines. Three moving fog layers cover the sketch while waiting. The finished image begins a 2.7-second reveal only after its image element loads. Original canvas pixels are hidden while the manifestation is visible and recoverable through Back to sketch. Errors and cancellation clear the waiting state, preserve the original, and retain retry access. Reduced-motion preference disables spell movement and fades. Physical animation quality and energy impact on DC-1 remain manual checks; the existing owner report of good pen latency predates this animation layer.

The typewriter opens a modal speech bubble. Its text field is focused synchronously from the tap so a mobile browser can open its keyboard. VisualViewport resize/scroll adjusts its inset around an on-screen keyboard. Closing it retains the shared saved spell. The small sparkle control submits the same image API request as the other cast methods. Native keyboard appearance is not proved by a desktop focus test.

Tools, undo/redo, downloads, new page, prior images, and settings are in the desk drawer. The old room-illustration toggle was removed because it controlled obsolete sidebars. The model, fidelity prompt, provider routing, original sketches and gallery persistence were not changed for this redesign.

Validation: evidence/qa-immersive and evidence/qa-immersive-typewriter contain the affected profile results. Browser tests exercise gestures, typed casts, interruptions, failure recovery, original-image preservation, historical reroll source, pixel erasing, six layouts, reduced motion and keyboard-sized bubble layout. Animation screenshots from scripts/immersive-preview.ts use a prior synthetic tournament result with a mocked network response; they are not a new live provider test.

Owner live trial: on 2026-09-11 UTC the owner reported a successful complete cast on the connected DC-1. The device screenshots showed a sketched person at left, dog lower right, and sun upper right; the returned image retained that composition and added heat-related details. Practice scribbles and handwriting were also preserved. This confirms that particular real cast on the previous deployed interface, not the new frame/animation/typewriter. The screenshots were inspected from temporary local copies and were not added to public-repository fixtures.

Final deployment: 739e3765-d65a-44d6-95be-0cdddf8f244e on the existing Railway URL. Hosted frame/typewriter loading and typed-field focus passed in evidence/immersive-deployment.json. The initial combined browser run passed 20 of 21 cases; the early-release test used coordinates outside the new rounded wand hit target. It was corrected to press the center and first assert that connection setup actually starts. The focused rerun passed. The complete final rerun is recorded separately in evidence/qa-immersive-final, retaining the first failing report for provenance.
