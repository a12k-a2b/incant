## Intent
Incant is an immersive wizard desk, primarily for a Daylight DC-1 Android tablet (1184×1584 portrait and corresponding landscape). Most space is a drawing parchment, with illustrated frame objects as controls rather than a toolbar. The sketch establishes composition, proportions, positions and key subjects. Voice or typing describes details and rendering: the result should communicate the user's pictured idea rather than invent a replacement composition. Recasting the same sketch with different spells is a core playful feature. Stylus pixel erasing, touch/palm rejection, native-feeling gestures, low visual distraction and discoverability matter.

The wand supports hold/speak/release AND tap/speak/tap. Connection, recording, final transcription, strike, fog, image reveal and cancellation should form one coherent truthful sequence. Optional conversational voice is configured in dragon settings; dictation remains default. Typewriter permits quiet use. Moon safely starts a new sketch. Small parchment compares original/result. Spellbooks preserve sketch bundles with all generations, sessions and days; local durability and Railway backup matter. Owl shares image + original sketch + actual spell, with a short playful title/body; optional receipt/reply links must be honest about what they prove. No envelope overlay on owl. Dragon/settings, share tray, library should match parchment/ink/wax design. Onboarding is skippable and replayable. Big/balanced and see-through visual choices are rollback options. Do not implement v2 story-style continuity, four-style exploration or storm-cloud rendering now.

## Archetypes (review all, prioritize by impact)
1. Curious first-time nontechnical DC-1 user: discovers controls, knows when to speak, succeeds without instruction or accidental menus.
2. Fluent stylus sketcher / palm-heavy left-handed user: pen, eraser, hover, touch, landscape, overlay hit areas, resize and keyboard interruptions.
3. Communication-focused storyteller: sketch identity across recasts, late/long speech, conversational voice, typing, exact intent/source/prompt association.
4. Impatient user on weak Wi-Fi: rapid taps, early release, interruption, cancel, retry, offline, background/foreground, permission failure and stale completions.
5. Keeper of valuable creations / party host: hours of sketches, multi-generation bundles, reload, app update, backup restore/retry, storage failure and no silent loss.
6. Sender and recipient: native share activation, correct attachments and prompt, offline/quota conditions, opened-versus-delivered truth, access isolation, untrusted images and replies.
7. Immersive/accessibility critic: contrast/grayscale, touch targets, keyboard/screen-reader use, reduced motion, font/loading, menus and consistency with the room.
