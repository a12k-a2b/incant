Inspected all eight final comparison PNGs directly, alongside fixture spells, anchor notes, and frozen P04/P07 templates. This is visual judgment of synthetic fixtures, not a device or production reliability measurement.

Provisional preference: P04 (Grok minimal). Differences are small and do not establish universal superiority.

The handle failures matter to the product. One of four teapot outputs preserves an open handle; zero of seven screening mug outputs preserved the overlapping handle. Prompt-only adherence is imperfect, especially with ambiguous overlapping outlines. Keep original sketch recoverable and provide retries; do not market this as exact tracing. A future benchmark should include real owner sketches and explicit local correction spells.

No pixel-registration scores or statistical significance are claimed. Fable supplies rubric scores independently. P04's smaller template also makes future user-directed local edits easier to reason about; that is a tie-breaker, not empirical proof.

## Reconciliation after Fable's independent review

Fable also selects P04, but correctly calls out an ambiguity in our fixture metadata: the teapot sketch has no drawn inner handle contour. My first reading treated the pentagon as an implied open loop; a solid shape is also supported by the actual marks. The fixture anchor's “open hole” is therefore not reliable ground truth. Withdraw the claim that the three solid teapot handles are established failures; retain the disagreement as an ambiguous interpretation case. The screening mug has an explicitly named mug and overlapping handle outline, so its repeated omission remains a useful concern, though overlap makes that fixture difficult too.

Selection remains P04 on the combined evidence and its slightly better boat placement. Fable means: P04 92.25, P07 90.125. This small difference is not statistically established.
